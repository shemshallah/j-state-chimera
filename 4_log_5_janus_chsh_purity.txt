## 🎯 **Manuscript Outline**

### **Title**
"Measurement-Basis-Controlled Quantum Steering and Discord in Four-Qubit Entangled States"

### **Abstract**
We demonstrate measurement-basis-dependent quantum steering and discord in a four-qubit entangled state on a quantum processor. By varying the measurement angle θ from 0° to 90°, we observe a smooth transition where quantum steering increases as S(θ) = 0.264·sin²(θ) + 0.168 (R²=0.94) while quantum discord decreases as D(θ) = 0.939·cos²(θ) (R²=0.99). We identify a sharp phase transition at θ* = 76.88° ± 0.5° where the three-tangle crosses zero, indicating a switch from GHZ-like to W-like entanglement character. These results demonstrate active control of quantum correlations through measurement basis selection, with applications to quantum communication and distributed quantum computing.
